Andrea's Spicy Pico de Gallo

Total Time: 30 Minutes       Feeds: 15 People

Ingredients

  • 5 Tomatoes
  • 8 Jalapeños
  • 1 Bushel of Cilantro
  • 2 White onions
  • 8 Limes
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Instructions

  1. Dice tomatoes into small pieces.
  2. Dice Jalapeños into small pieces. De-seeding is optional based on your preference of heat.
  3. Finely chop cilantro.
  4. Dice Onions into small pieces.
  5. Cut limes into halves, squeeze juice into big bowl.
  6. Add all chopped ingredients into bowl with lime juicce
  7. Add 1 1/2 table spoon of salt.
  8. Add 1 table spoon of pepper.
  9. Stir all ingredients until thoroughly mixed. Add additional salt or pepper if needed.
  10. Serve alongside preferred chips and enjoy!






















































Andrea's Aguachiles

Total Time: 1 Day (1 hour prep time and 23hrs in Fridge)       Feeds: 5-8 People

Ingredients

  • 20 decent sized limes
  • 1 bushel of Cilantro
  • 2 Purple Onions
  • 2 Cucumbers
  • salt
  • pepper
  • Onion powder
  • Minced Garlic
  • 4 Serrano Peppers
  • 6 Jalapeños
  • 4 Tomatillos
  • 1lb of raw de-veined shrimp

Instructions

  1. Cut limes in halves and squeeze juice in large bowl.
  2. Finely chop cilantro.
  3. Slice onions into thin wedge pieces.
  4. Peel cucumbers and cut into half-circles.
  5. Roast serrano and jalapeño peppers and tomatillos until skin blisters. Should be about 5 minutes each side.
  6. Peel skin off all peppers and tomatillos and liquify into blender.
  7. Add contents from blender into bowl with lime juice and add 2 table spoons of Salt and Pepper with 1 table spoon of Onion powder.
  8. Add the rest of the diced ingredients into the bowl with the raw shrimp and stir thoroughly.
  9. Place plastic wrap to seal the bowl.
  10. Let rest in fridge overnight so lime and salt can cure the shrimp.
  11. Once dish has finished resting in fridge, pare dish with tostadas and enjoy!






















































Andrea's Mexican Rice

Cook Time: 40 Minutes       Feeds: 5-7 People

Ingredients

  • Jasmine Rice
  • 1/2 of a White Onion
  • 1 Roma Tomatoe
  • 1/3 of a bushel of Cilantro
  • 2 ribs of celery
  • 3 cloves of garlic
  • 1 1/2 cans of tomato sauce
  • Salt
  • Knorrs Caldo de Tomate con Sabor de Pollo Seasoning
  • Canola Oil
  • 1 1/2 cup of water

Instructions

  1. Dice tomato, onion, and celery.
  2. Finely chop cilantro.
  3. Mince cloves of garlic.
  4. Turn burner to a low to medium heat setting, generously drizzle canola oil in a sauce pan.
  5. Once the oil is warmed, pour 1 1/2 cups of rice
  6. Check/stir periodically until rice turns golden brown. Also, add in garlic, tomato, celery,cilantro, and onion to sauté.
  7. Once rice is golden brown and vegetables have turned translucent, pour in 1 1/2 can of tomatoe sauce and water
  8. Add in generous table spoon of Knorrs tomato buoillon and salt.
  9. Mix ingredients and bump heat setting up to a strong and steady medium flame, closing suace pan with lid.
  10. Routinely check on rice slightly lifting lid. Once water starts evaporating but rice is not full softened, you may add 1/3 cup of water to pan as needed.
  11. Once rice is fully softened and has fluffed up, turn off burner and remove pan from heat.
  12. Let rice rest for a few minutes by lifting lid to saucepan and allowing steam to settle slowly, then enjoy!
